## 6. How to Fill the Walmart Seller Application Form

When submitting your application on **[marketplace.walmart.com](https://marketplace.walmart.com)**:
1. **Primary Website / eCommerce URL:** Enter your deployed website URL (e.g., `https://texasexpotech.com`).
2. **Categories You Intend to Sell:** Select:
   - *Apparel & Accessories*
   - *Home & Home Improvement*
   - *Consumer Electronics & Accessories*
   - *Kitchen & Dining*
3. **Fulfillment Method:** Select **Self-Fulfilled / 3PL (FedEx / UPS Ground - 2 to 5 Days)**.
4. **Integration Type:** Select **Direct eCommerce Storefront / Custom API**.
5. **Business Address & Phone:** Enter the exact address and phone displayed on your website.
